Privacy

Short, because there is little to say. This site collects no personal data, sets no cookies, and has no account to create.

What the website records

Each page view sends a small event so we can tell whether anyone is reading this and which link brought them. The event contains: the page path, the referring site's hostname or a campaign label from the URL, whether the device is a phone or a desktop, the browser's two-letter language, how long the page was in the foreground, how far it was scrolled, and which buttons were clicked.

It also contains a random identifier generated fresh on every page load. It is not stored in your browser, is not linked to anything, and is gone when you close the tab. It exists only so two events from the same visit can be counted as one visit.

No IP address is stored. No cookie is set. No profile is built. No identifier follows you between visits or between sites. There is no advertising network on this page and no third-party tracker. That is why there is no consent banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Events are sent to a topic on the public relay ntfy.sh, which holds messages for about twelve hours before discarding them, and are aggregated into counts. Your browser also contacts GitHub, which serves this site and will see your IP address as any web server does, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

If you buy something

Checkout is hosted by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never touch this site or any server of ours. Stripe tells us that a payment succeeded, the amount, the country, and the email address you gave for the receipt. We use that email only to answer you or to issue a refund, and we do not add you to any mailing list, because there is no mailing list.

We keep a record of each purchase, which is an accounting obligation, for as long as tax law requires. We hold no card data at any point.

What the tools you download do

The scanner and the Fix Pack generator run entirely on your own machine. They make no network requests, send us nothing, and work offline. The scanner's source is public, so you need not take our word for it.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to it being held, by writing to hugo@vystral.com. In practice, for a visitor who has not bought anything, the honest answer is that we hold nothing that could identify you. If you are in the European Economic Area and are not satisfied with the response, you may complain to your national data protection authority.
