What it does not do

This is not a security audit, a certification, or a guarantee that your application is secure. It checks a specific and common set of mistakes about who can reach your data. It does not review your application logic, your dependencies, your infrastructure, or anything else, and a clean result does not mean you are safe.

The generated SQL changes access to your database. Turning on Row Level Security denies access until a policy allows it, so parts of your application can stop working until the policies are right. You are expected to read the SQL before running it and to apply it to a branch or a copy first. The report tells you which tables the generator was unsure about.
